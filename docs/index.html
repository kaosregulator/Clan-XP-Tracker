Track your clan's XP, streaks & warnings — right inside Discord.

ClanXP lets officers log members' XP day by day, roll it up into weekly and monthly totals, send reminders and warnings, and see everything on a manager-style calendar. No spreadsheets, no leaving Discord.

What it does

Daily XP ledger

Officers enter a member's XP for a day; ClanXP rolls it up into week, month and all-time totals automatically — and past days can be backfilled.

Calendar & history

A per-member monthly time card shows each day as done or missed against a daily target, so you can spot who's slipping at a glance.

Reminders & warnings

Nudge members who are behind, or issue tracked warnings for missed XP — posted to your channels with a full, verifiable log.

Officer controls

Per-server setup for goals, roles, channels and schedules, plus a weekly review panel and a dashboard of who needs attention.
