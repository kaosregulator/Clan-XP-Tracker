Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This policy explains what data the ClanXP Discord bot ("ClanXP", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it is handled. By adding ClanXP to a Discord server or using its features, you agree to this policy.

1. Who this applies to

ClanXP is a tool that server administrators ("officers") add to their own Discord servers to track member activity. When you are a member of a server that uses ClanXP, the server's officers act as the controllers of your data; ClanXP processes it on their behalf.

2. What we collect

ClanXP only stores what it needs to provide activity tracking. This includes:

CategoryExamples
Discord identifiers User IDs, usernames and display names, avatar image URLs, server (guild) IDs, role IDs, and channel IDs.
Activity & XP data XP amounts entered per day, weekly/monthly totals, streaks, completion status, and per-day vacation marks.
Enforcement records Reminders sent, warnings issued (including the reason text an officer types and who issued them).
Submissions (if used) Links to proof screenshots members post, and any notes attached to them.
Configuration Per-server settings you choose: goals, daily targets, tracked/exempt roles, channels, timezone and schedule.
Audit logs A record of officer actions (who changed what, and when) so servers have an accountable history.
Website sessions If you sign in to the optional companion website with Discord, a login session and your Discord account's basic profile.

ClanXP does not collect your email address, phone number, password, payment information, private message content, or messages from channels it was not explicitly configured to use.

3. How we use it

  • To record and display XP, streaks, calendars, reminders and warnings.
  • To send reminders and warnings that officers request, in the channels they configure.
  • To let officers configure and operate the bot for their server.
  • To keep an audit trail so servers can verify who took which action.
  • To operate, secure, debug and improve the service.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How it's shared

Data entered in a server is visible to that server's officers and, where relevant (e.g. reminder or warning messages), to members of that server. We do not share data with third parties except infrastructure providers that host the bot and its database on our behalf, and where required by law. Discord itself processes the messages and interactions per its own Privacy Policy.

5. Retention

Data is kept while ClanXP is active in your server so history and calendars remain available. When the bot is removed from a server, or on request, the associated data can be deleted (see below). Some minimal logs may persist briefly for security and debugging before being rotated out.

6. Your choices & deletion

  • Members: ask an officer of your server to remove or correct your records, or to exclude you from tracking.
  • Officers: removing ClanXP from your server stops all further collection. To request deletion of your server's stored data, contact us using the details below.

7. Children

ClanXP is not directed at children. You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement for your country to use Discord and, by extension, ClanXP.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect stored data. No system is perfectly secure, but access is limited to what is needed to run the service.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of ClanXP after a change means you accept the revised policy.
